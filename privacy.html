Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who operates this app

This app is a private email automation tool operated by Inta Smilga for personal use. Contact: smilgainta@gmail.com

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail accounts to read email messages and download attachments (such as invoices).

How data is used

Data is used only to summarize and analyze the owner's emails and to store invoices for the owner's own bookkeeping. Data is processed in the owner's own n8n automation environment.

Data sharing

Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
